#### Improvements

- Brightness OSD, bar popup slider and dashboard widget now update instantly when brightness changes from anywhere — `XF86MonBrightnessUp` / `Down`, the idle pre-lock dim, or any external `axctl brightness …` call — not only from in-shell controls. The shell now subscribes to axctl's `Event.BrightnessChanged` over the daemon socket and routes each event into the matching `BrightnessMonitor`. The five-second `list` poll stays as a safety net for monitor hot-plug and bus re-detection.

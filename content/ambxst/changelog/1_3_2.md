### CLIPBOARD OVERHAUL

#### Changes

- Clipboard history now lives in two **encrypted SQLite stores** (Adiantum, pure Go, per-installation key), with images stored as blobs and legacy items migrated automatically. Unpinned history is capped at **50 items** (pinned exempt).
- New **Move to /tmp** toggle in Settings → System: unpinned history lives in tmpfs and is wiped on reboot.

#### Fixes

- Pomodoro no longer freezes the countdown after editing the timer duration, and the alarm blink works again. Thanks [callenflynn](https://github.com/callenflynn) for [PR #235](https://github.com/Axenide/Ambxst/pull/235), fixing [#77](https://github.com/Axenide/Ambxst/issues/77).

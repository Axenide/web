### MODS ON READ-ONLY SOURCES

#### Fixes

- **Mods now work on NixOS**: composing a mod copied the Nix store's read-only permissions into the build directory, so enabling a mod failed with `permission denied`. Staging copies now get normal writable permissions, keeping the store untouched.
- Stale generations rebuild automatically: after an Ambxst update that moves or replaces the base source, the daemon recomposes the active generation on startup instead of waiting for a manual **Rebuild** in the Mods panel. If the rebuild fails, the shell still starts from the base source and the panel keeps its manual rebuild banner.

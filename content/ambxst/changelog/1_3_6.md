### NIRI SUPPORT + WALLPAPER REWORK

**Ambxst now officially supports Niri**, made possible by axctl. Please test it and open an issue if you find problems.

#### Added

- **Niri support**.
- **New wallpaper engine**: video wallpapers and GIFs play natively through QtMultimedia (GStreamer backend), replacing mpvpaper ([#160](https://github.com/Axenide/Ambxst/issues/160)). The lockscreen now shows the live wallpaper too.
- **Built-in runtime translations**: flathead's [community.i18n mod](https://github.com/flathead/ambxst-mod-i18n) absorbed into the shell, no mod required.
- **`ambxst toggle bar`**: new CLI command and system bind to pin/unpin the bar (SUPER + SHIFT + B).

#### Fixes

- GIF animation on Qt 6.11.
- Compositor detection at startup.
- Workspace highlight on newly created workspaces.
- Assistant sidebar positioning ([#220](https://github.com/Axenide/Ambxst/issues/220)).

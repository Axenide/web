### UNIVERSAL MODIFIER-ALONE BINDS

**Ambxst 1.3.7 ships [axctl v0.0.24](https://github.com/Axenide/axctl)**, which brings keyd-style modifier-alone binds (e.g. Super alone opens the launcher) to every compositor, not just niri.

#### Fixed

- **Launcher opening and closing instantly**: on Hyprland (and MangoWC), pressing Super alone fired the command twice — once from the compositor bind and once from axctl's keymon monitor — so the launcher toggled itself off right away. Modifier-self binds are now skipped in every generated compositor config, and the keymon monitor is the single implementation for all compositors.

#### Upgrade notes

- The update **adds your user to the `input` group**, which axctl needs to read `/dev/input` events (sudo password may be prompted during install).
- The group change **only applies after logging out and back in** — restarting the daemon is not enough. Until then, Super-alone binds stay disabled.
- If you install manually, add the group yourself (`sudo usermod -aG input "$USER"`); on NixOS, add `input` to `users.users.<name>.extraGroups`.
- Check the monitor state anytime with `axctl system keymon-status`.

**Ambxst 1.3.9 ships [axctl v0.0.27](https://github.com/Axenide/axctl)**, which fixes stale fullscreen state in the compositor state dump.

#### Fixed

- **Fullscreen state leaking across monitors**: entering fullscreen on one monitor hid the bar and notch on every screen. The effect now applies only to the monitor that actually has the fullscreen window.
- **Fullscreen state updating late**: the cached state stayed stale until the next full refresh (typically a focus change), delaying the bar and notch update. It now refreshes immediately on every fullscreen event.
- Workspace app icons are now centered to the pixel.
- Screen recording toggles are matched by action name instead of the localized tooltip.
